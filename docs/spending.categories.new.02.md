On the 储藏 pages the group headers would match the 花费 二级 names. "零食饮品 is under 食品杂货" would then mean the same thing on both sides.

Migration

- Code only (no data changes): regrouping, renames, budget weights, and keeping old links and bookmarks working (e.g. cat=food → 食品杂货 + 餐饮).
- Data changes (a one-time SQL migration, safe to re-run):
  - Items under 日用品 with subcategory 洗护用品 → 个人护理. Purchases linked to those items (purchases.item_id) follow them.
  - Items under 干货粮油 with subcategory 零食坚果 or 冲饮 → 零食饮品, with their linked purchases.
  - Older purchases with no linked item stay where they are, because there's no way to tell what they were.
  - The migration also goes into schema.sql. No new tables, so backup is unaffected.
- Budgets: the old 餐饮 and 购物日用 budgets get split across the new 二级 using the last 6 months of spending, so nothing needs to be entered again.
- Imports:
  - Grocery stores → 食品杂货.
  - Costco, Sam's Club, Walmart, Target and Amazon → 综合购物（待细分）.
  - When you re-categorize a store, imports keep remembering it.

Decisions

1. Overall structure: one tree, where a 储藏 category is a 花费 三级 and the 储藏 pages group by 花费 二级. OK?
2. Split 购物日用 into four (日用个护 / 家居家电 / 服饰鞋包 / 数码电子) plus 综合购物（待细分）? Or keep fewer, e.g. merge 服饰 and 数码 into one 「购物」?
3. New 个人护理 category (洗护 split out of 日用品), and moving 理发美容 into it?
4. 零食饮品: merge 零食坚果 and 冲饮 in with 饮品? Or keep 饮品 as is?
5. 餐饮 三级: keep one category (外出就餐 / 外卖 / 咖啡), or split into 堂食 / 外卖 / 咖啡茶饮? Bank imports usually can't tell these apart, so I lean towards keeping one.
6. Costco and mixed receipts: default to 综合购物（待细分）, plus a 拆分 feature later? (This is the same as question 4 from my last message.)
7. Budget carry-over: split old budgets by the last 6 months of spending (recommended)?
